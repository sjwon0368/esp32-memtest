@NAME@ - prebuilt esp32-memtest
====================================

The simplest option is the single merged image, flashed at offset 0x0:

  esptool.py --chip @CHIP@ -p /dev/ttyUSB0 write_flash 0x0 @NAME@.bin

Hold BOOT, tap RESET and release BOOT if it cannot connect. Then watch the
output at 115200 baud, 8N1:

  minicom -D /dev/ttyUSB0 -b 115200

The port can also be /dev/ttyACM*, depending on the USB bridge fitted to your
board.

If you prefer the three separate images:

  esptool.py --chip @CHIP@ -p /dev/ttyUSB0 write_flash \
    0x0     bootloader.bin \
    0x8000  partition-table.bin \
    0x10000 @NAME@.bin

Then press a key on the finished screen: r runs the suite again, Enter prints
the full report, q exits.

About PSRAM
-----------

This build uses the shared defaults for @TARGET@. If your module has octal
PSRAM (an R8 part, e.g. ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 N16R8) but quad mode is selected, the
chip boot-loops before the tester starts with:

  E quad_psram: PSRAM chip is not connected, or wrong PSRAM line mode

The tester itself reports the PSRAM size it found on the startup screen, so if
that line says "none detected" while your board has PSRAM, this is almost always
the reason.
